There's a difference between someone who likes birds and someone who birds — binoculars by the back door, a running list of yard visitors, and strong opinions about squirrel-proof feeders. That second group is a genuinely underserved gift category; most "nature" merchandise is too generic to register as actually about birding.

Field-guide style, not cartoon birds

What tends to land with this crowd is art that respects the hobby — recognizable species, field-guide-accurate poses, a restrained color palette — rather than a cutesy cartoon cardinal. It signals the gift-giver actually gets it.
